The Last Bet: One More Spin

A pixel-art slot-machine roguelike. Start with one coin. Break the bank.

How to play

  • Pull the lever. Each pull costs the current stake, shown under the lever.
  • Match 3 or more symbols along a line, in a straight row, or 3 down one reel. Several wins in one pull stack a combo.
  • After every 7 pulls the store opens. Tap a card to buy a boon. Buying it again levels it up. Tap one of your slots to sell.
  • From round 2 each round brings a bane: a rule that works against you. Tap its badge (bottom left) to read it.
  • Tap ? during play for the paytable.
  • If you can't cover a pull, you go all in with what you have. You lose at 0 coins. You win at 100 quintillion.

FAQ

Is this real gambling? No. The Last Bet uses no real money. Coins exist only inside the game and can never be bought, cashed out or exchanged for anything of value.

Does it need the internet? No. It plays fully offline.

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

The game feels too hard. Winning depends on building an engine. Try committing to one family of boons, like fruit and cherries, instead of picking at random.
