Privacy Policy

The Last Bet: One More Spin · Last updated 17 November 2026

Short version: the game collects no personal data. Nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your saved run, settings and statistics (such as your best round) are stored locally on your device so you can continue playing. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

No third-party SDKs, ad networks or analytics services are included in the app.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if an online leaderboard is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will be updated to match.
